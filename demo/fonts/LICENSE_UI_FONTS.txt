                  File: JetBrainsMono-Medium.ttf

The Inter and JetBrains Mono files are static instances made with fontTools (varLib.instancer) from the upstream
variable fonts at fixed weights (400/500/600/700 and 400/500), so the menu can load fixed weights without variable
axes. Glyph outlines are not otherwise changed. Under OFL 1.1 these are Modified Versions; neither font declares a
Reserved Font Name, so the family names are kept. They remain under OFL 1.1 and may not be sold by themselves.
